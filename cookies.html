Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how www.alexamotors.com uses cookies and similar technologies, in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR.

The short version

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not allow third parties to place them on our website.

What we store on your device

NameTypePurposeHow long
am_noticeLocal storage (strictly necessary)Remembers that you have seen our cookie notice, so it is not shown on every page.Until you clear your browser data

Because this item is strictly necessary to provide the notice you asked to dismiss, it does not require consent under PECR. It contains no personal information.

Third-party services

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Google Fonts does not set cookies, but your browser connects to Google’s servers to download the fonts, which shares your IP address with Google. Our enquiry forms send your message through FormSubmit, which does not set cookies on our website. See our Privacy Policy for more about these providers.

Managing cookies and storage

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. Blocking them will not stop our website from working; the cookie notice will simply appear again.
