Buying guide

Before you take the wheel

A few things worth checking when a car catches your eye.

What should I check on a used car?

Ask for the service history, check the mileage against the paperwork, and inspect the tyres, lights and bodywork.

What should I look for on a test drive?

Try the car at different speeds if the route allows. Check visibility, seating comfort, braking and any warning lights.

How do I compare running costs?

Compare insurance quotes, fuel or charging costs and servicing needs for the specific cars on your shortlist.
