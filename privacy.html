Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Alexa Motors Ltd (“we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information when you visit www.alexamotors.com or contact us. We are the controller of this information under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact us

For any question about this policy or your personal information, email contact@alexamotors.com.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you give us through our forms or by email: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the model you are interested in, your budget, how you prefer to be contacted and your message.
  • Technical information that any website receives when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. Our hosting and font providers process this to deliver the site.

We do not use advertising or analytics tracking, and we do not build marketing profiles.

How and why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, answer questions about vehicles and arrange viewings — because you have asked us to, and to take steps before a possible purchase (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the website running securely — our legitimate interest in operating a safe website (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet legal obligations arising from a purchase (Article 6(1)(c)).

We will not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the website and handle email. They process information only to provide their service to us:

ProviderWhat they do
FormSubmit (formsubmit.co)Delivers messages sent through our enquiry forms to our inbox.
Namecheap (Private Email)Hosts our email mailbox and domain.
GitHub PagesHosts the website and may keep short-term server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
Google FontsSupplies the fonts used on the site; your browser connects to Google to download them, which shares your IP address with Google.

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, the United States. Where information leaves the UK, it is protected by safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up. If you buy a vehicle from us, we keep the records the law requires us to keep for that transaction. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it;
  • ask us to transfer information you gave us to another organisation.

To use any of these rights, email contact@alexamotors.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first, so please contact us.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
